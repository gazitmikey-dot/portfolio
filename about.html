About me

Hi, I'm Michal Gazit Shprung, a UX/UI product designer who turns complex platforms into intuitive experiences. I'm a mother of three based in Beit Yehoshua, Israel.

With over 9 years of design experience and a background in law, I specialize in simplifying complex workflows across sophisticated platforms — including compliance tools, cloud security monitoring solutions, and AI-powered compliance questionnaires. As the Product UX/UI Lead at Findings, I design end-to-end experiences that bring clarity, structure, and usability to complex enterprise products.

I lead projects from research through development, working closely with product, engineering, and customer success teams to ensure solutions are practical, scalable, and aligned with real user needs.

AI is part of how I design every day. I work with Claude Code directly in our production codebase — reviewing features as they're built, prototyping new directions inside the real product instead of static mockups, and keeping our Figma design system aligned with the components engineering actually ships. When a fix is small, I ship it myself: I push UI changes to production. It shortens the distance between a design decision and a live product, and makes me a closer partner to the engineering team.

I have a deep love for visual art and design, which consistently inspires my creative approach. I'm always looking to grow — and would love to hear from you.
